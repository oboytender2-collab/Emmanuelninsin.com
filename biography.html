MY BIOGRAPHY

The story is still being written.

Selected chapters from Emmanuel Osei Kojo Ninsin’s ongoing life story, presented as a working biography.

01

Childhood, School and the Pursuit of Excellence

From Ntontom M/A Basic School to Praprababida M/A JHS, this chapter follows the early academic journey, the determination to compete among the best students, and the lessons learned along the way.

02

University Practice SHS — Challenges, Mentors and the Birth of a Dream

University Practice SHS brought new opportunities, financial challenges, friendships, leadership experiences and the influence of mentors including Khaptain, Pay Money and Slym. It also marked an important stage in the development of the ambition to study law and pursue justice.

03

UCC CODE — Working Draft

The current chapter follows the return to tertiary education through the University of Cape Coast College of Distance Education, including study-centre life, assignments, group work, examinations, academic growth and the continuing journey in Social Studies.

Working biography: Additional chapters will be added as the story continues.
