ABOUT ME

A journey shaped by education, service and purpose.

My name is Emmanuel Osei Kojo Ninsin. My life journey is one of education, perseverance, mentorship, faith and purpose. From my early years at Ntontom M/A Basic School and later Praprababida M/A JHS, I developed a strong desire to learn and to excel. I was often among the best students in my class, and I grew up with the determination to keep pushing forward even when the road became difficult.

My secondary-school years at University Practice SHS became another important chapter of my story. I studied General Arts, with subjects including Geography, Economics, CRS and Government. Those years brought academic experiences, financial challenges, friendships, leadership opportunities and lessons about fairness and justice. They also introduced me to people whose mentorship and support had a lasting influence on my life, particularly Sir Andy Amo Smith (Khaptain), Francis Ogoe (Pay Money), and my young uncle Paul Ninsin (Slym).

After SHS, my journey did not immediately move to university. I spent a period at home and worked, including as a mobile money agent and DSR for Ramstel Telecom. An encounter with Mr. Seba eventually encouraged me to apply to the University of Cape Coast's College of Distance Education. That opportunity opened another chapter of my educational journey.

I am now pursuing a Bachelor of Education in Social Studies at UCC CODE. Teaching, leadership and service remain important parts of my development. At the same time, my experiences have strengthened my ambition to study law and become a lawyer committed to justice, fairness, human rights and access to justice for people who may otherwise struggle to be heard.

My story is still being written. The journey from childhood to SHS and now university has taught me that setbacks do not have to define a person's future. Education, discipline, good mentorship and perseverance can help turn difficult beginnings into a meaningful purpose.
