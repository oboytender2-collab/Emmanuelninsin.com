MY STORY

From childhood dreams to a bigger stage.

My story is one of ambition, setbacks, mentorship, persistence, faith and continued growth. The journey is not over; every new chapter adds to the story of the person I am becoming.

BIOGRAPHY

Biography

My story is one of ambition, setbacks, mentorship, persistence, faith and continued growth. I began my educational journey at Ntontom M/A Basic School and later attended Praprababida M/A JHS. From an early age, I was determined to perform well and was often among the top two students in my class. My mother's record of coming first throughout her own basic education inspired me to work hard and build a future through learning.

My journey has included challenges, periods of uncertainty, supportive mentors and opportunities to serve others. After senior high school, I spent approximately two years at home while looking for a way forward. During that period, I worked as a mobile money agent and a Direct Sales Representative for Ramstel Telecom. These experiences taught me responsibility, patience and how to relate to people from different backgrounds. My story is still unfolding, and each new chapter continues to shape the person I am becoming.

EDUCATION

My Educational Journey

Ntontom M/A Basic School

My formal education began at Ntontom M/A Basic School. I was motivated to do well and often found myself among the top two students. At primary level, I received recognition for English reading in the Kyekyewere Circuit. My mother's strong academic example encouraged me to take education seriously.

Praprababida M/A JHS

Junior high school challenged me to become more disciplined and competitive. I worked hard to perform well, and experiences during revision week taught me important lessons about preparation, responsibility and perseverance. I completed the BECE with aggregate 12, an achievement I valued greatly.

University Practice Senior High School

I attended University Practice SHS in Cape Coast, where I studied General Arts with Geography, Economics, Christian Religious Studies and Government. Although Free SHS helped with tuition, the cost of books and other school needs remained difficult for my family. Mentors including Sir Andy Amo Smith (Khaptain), Francis Ogoe (Pay Money), and my uncle Paul Ninsin (Slym) encouraged and supported my growth. My experiences in school, including serving as Grounds Prefect, taught me lessons about leadership, fairness and proper procedures.

University of Cape Coast — College of Distance Education

After working for a period, I was encouraged by Mr. Seba to consider the University of Cape Coast's College of Distance Education (UCC CODE). I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) in Social Studies. University life has challenged me through assignments, group work, examinations and demanding courses, while helping me grow academically and personally. My studies in Sociology, Economics, Geography, Government and educational foundations continue to deepen my understanding of people, institutions and society. This journey remains ongoing.

Future Educational Goals

After completing my current studies, I hope to pursue law in Ghana and continue developing the knowledge and skills needed to contribute meaningfully to society.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Achievements and Leadership

My achievements reflect persistence, learning and a willingness to serve. They include recognition for English reading at primary level in the Kyekyewere Circuit, completing the BECE with aggregate 12, continuing my education at UCC CODE after a period away from school, and gaining practical work experience while finding a path back to education.

In 2026, I was elected DESAG-UCC Central Regional Welfare Officer for the 2026/2027 academic year and was sworn into office on 10 October 2026 at the UCC CODE Conference Centre. I regard this responsibility as an opportunity to listen to students, communicate their concerns and contribute to their welfare. I hope to keep building on these milestones through service, integrity and continued growth.

TEACHING

My Passion for Teaching

Teaching is an important part of my educational and professional journey. My observation and off-campus teaching experience at University Practice SHS has helped me appreciate the preparation, patience, fairness and responsibility required of an educator.

I am interested in teaching Social Studies, Economics, Geography and other subjects related to Social Studies. I enjoy helping learners connect classroom knowledge with real-life issues, understand society and develop their own ability to think critically. I hope to keep improving my teaching skills and contribute positively to the education of young people.

LAW AND JUSTICE

My Aspiration to Pursue Law

My interest in law grows from my concern for fairness, human rights and the treatment of people in society. I hope to study law in Ghana and work toward becoming a lawyer who stands for justice, particularly for people who are less privileged and may struggle to obtain legal support.

The issues that matter to me include access to justice, fair trials, discrimination and equal treatment under the law. I believe that education, integrity, careful listening and a commitment to service are important foundations for this ambition. My legal journey is a future goal, and I intend to prepare for it through my current studies and continued learning.
