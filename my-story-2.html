Untold Story

My Educational Journey

My educational journey began at Ntontom M/A Basic School and continued at Praprababida M/A JHS. I later attended University Practice Senior High School, where I studied General Arts.

I am continuing my tertiary education at the University of Cape Coast, College of Distance Education, pursuing a Bachelor of Education in Social Studies. My journey is still ongoing, and I remain committed to learning and personal development.

Leadership and Achievements

My interest in leadership is rooted in service, fairness, communication and the welfare of others. I was elected DESAG-UCC Central Regional Welfare Officer for the 2026/2027 term and sworn into office on 10 October 2026.

My Interest in Law and Justice

I aspire to study law and contribute to the promotion of human rights, fair trials and access to justice, especially for people who face disadvantages.

My story is still unfolding. I believe that education, perseverance and service can help me make a meaningful contribution to society.
