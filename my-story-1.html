Biography

My name is Emmanuel Osei Ninsin. My life story is a journey of learning, perseverance, faith, leadership, and determination to build a meaningful future.

My experiences have taught me that a person's background should not determine the limits of their dreams. Through education, discipline, and the support of people who believe in me, I continue to work towards my goals.

My story is still unfolding. Every new experience presents an opportunity to learn, grow, serve others, and make a difference.

My Educational Journey

My educational journey began at Ntontom M/A Basic School. From my early years, I worked hard to maintain a strong academic record and build a foundation for my future.

I later attended Praprababida M/A JHS, where competition, discipline, resilience, and preparation shaped my academic experience. I completed the Basic Education Certificate Examination with an aggregate of 12.

I continued at University Practice Senior High School in Cape Coast, studying General Arts. My elective subjects included Geography, Economics, Christian Religious Studies, and Government.

My SHS journey came with financial challenges and limited access to some learning materials. Mentors such as Sir Andy Amo Smith, known as Khaptain, and Francis Ogoe, known as Pay Money, helped shape my outlook on education and life.

After senior high school, I spent time working before continuing my education at the University of Cape Coast College of Distance Education (UCC CODE), where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Education in Social Studies.

My tertiary journey continues to strengthen my understanding of society, governance, education, and leadership.

My Achievements and Leadership

I believe achievement is not only about personal success but also about using opportunities to serve others.

I contested for the position of DESAG-UCC Central Regional Welfare Officer, campaigning for student welfare, inclusion, effective communication, and the needs of distance-learning students.

I was elected Central Regional Welfare Officer for the 2026/2027 academic year and sworn into office on 10 October 2026 at the UCC CODE Conference Centre.

This role gives me an opportunity to listen to students, promote their welfare, and contribute to a supportive student community.

My Dream of Law and Justice

Beyond my current studies, I aspire to study law and become a lawyer committed to justice, fairness, and the protection of human rights.

I am particularly concerned about access to justice for disadvantaged people, unfair trials, and discrimination. I hope to use my future legal education and professional experience to advocate for people whose voices are often overlooked.

To me, justice should not be a privilege reserved for the powerful. Everyone deserves fairness, dignity, and the opportunity to be heard.

Faith and Personal Values

My faith is an important part of my life. As one of Jehovah's Witnesses, I value the guidance of the Bible and seek to apply its principles in my daily conduct.

Honesty, humility, perseverance, respect, and love for others are values I strive to uphold as I pursue my goals.
