Biography

My journey is shaped by learning, perseverance, faith, service and the determination to keep moving forward. My experiences continue to teach me the value of humility, hard work and hope.

Achievements

Each milestone represents effort, growth and a willingness to accept responsibility. My educational progress and student leadership journey remain important parts of my story.

In October 2026, I was sworn in as DESAG-UCC Central Regional Welfare Officer for the 2026/2027 term.

Education

My education journey began in basic school, continued through University Practice Senior High School, and led to my ongoing Bachelor of Education in Social Studies studies at the University of Cape Coast College of Distance Education.

My tertiary journey is still ongoing, and I look forward to the lessons and opportunities ahead.

Law and Justice

I aspire to study law and use my knowledge to promote fairness, human rights and access to justice, particularly for people who may struggle to have their voices heard.
